# MacroFactor-October-PowerBI-Excel

### Data Information
---
**Data** - My own personal MacroFactor data that consisted of 31 days of daily macro summaries and a food log of each entry. 

**Data Cleaning & Analysis** - Microsoft Excel

**Data Visualization** - PowerBI

### Questions
---
* Was there a difference in caloric intake depending on the day of the week?
* Was there a difference in caloric intake depending on the timing of the day?
* What was the total amount of weight lost (trend) over the course of October?
* How many of the days was I on target (<=10%+ target calories)?
* What was my average expenditure during October?
* What was my average daily calories during October?
* Which foods were most commonly eaten?

### Summary of Findings
---
* There was a difference in the average amount of calories eaten during the week vs. the weekend.
* Breakfast was the largest (and most important!) meal of the day at 34.74% while dinner typically averaged 15.62% of daily calories.
* 7.14 lbs.
* 32% over the course of October I was considedred 'on target'.
* My average expenditure over October was 2,910 (kcal)
* My average calories over October were 2,084 (kcal)
* The top 5 most commonly eaten foods during October consist of Chicken Breast, Fudgesicles, Powdered Peanut Butter, Rice Krispies Treats and Vanilla Almond Milk.
